Process description

How the packaged STP works

The process stages below follow the treatment sequence. Final process design should be confirmed for the specific plant model and wastewater.

01

Solid Separation & Settling

Incoming domestic sewage enters the first chamber. Heavier suspended solids settle by gravity and are retained as sludge, reducing solids entering biological stages.

02

De-nitrification Chamber

Under low-oxygen conditions, organic material is decomposed and recirculated nitrate/nitrite can be converted into nitrogen gas, helping reduce total nitrogen.

03

Aerobic Chamber

Air supplied through the aeration system supports aerobic microorganisms that break down organic material, reducing BOD and other pollutants while producing biological sludge.

04

Clarifier & Recirculation

Suspended biological solids settle. Settled sludge containing active microorganisms is recirculated to maintain biomass and support treatment efficiency.

05

Chlorination Chamber

A controlled chlorine dose disinfects the water. Reuse or discharge depends on achieved quality, applicable standards and project requirements.
